SOURCE:
American Academy of Dermatology - How to Find Skin Cancer
https://www.aad.org/public/diseases/skin-cancer/find

New, changing, different, itching, or bleeding spots on the skin should
receive appropriate professional evaluation.

Skin cancer can occur in people of different skin colors.

Self-examination can help people notice new or changing lesions, but
self-examination and photographs cannot establish a definitive diagnosis.

A dermatologist is the appropriate specialist for evaluation of suspicious
skin lesions.

When skin cancer is suspected, biopsy and microscopic examination may be
needed to determine whether cancer cells are present.

Skin protection from ultraviolet radiation is an important preventive measure.
